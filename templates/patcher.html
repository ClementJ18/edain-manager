Edain Engine Patcher

Upload your ROTWK game.dat (build 2.01.2614.37001), pick the pySAGE patches you want, and get a patched copy back. These patches are engine-level: they apply to any ROTWK install of that build and benefit every mod on it. A few patches change the launcher or Worldbuilder instead of the engine, and ask for that file as well when you pick one. A patched engine also comes with the .sagepatch describing the INI it now accepts, for sage_lint. Your uploads are never modified and are deleted as soon as they have been read; the patched copies are kept for {{ ttl_minutes }} minutes so you can download them, then deleted as well. Keep the unpatched originals.
